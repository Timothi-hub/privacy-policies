Privacy Policy for PillTime

Last updated: November 2026

PillTime is designed as a personal medicine reminder application. This policy details how our application handles your information.

1. Data Collection & Privacy

PillTime stores medication schedules, dosages, and alarm times locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, or disclose personal health data to external servers or third parties.

2. App Permissions

The app requests notification and exact alarm permissions strictly to alert you about your scheduled medicine times.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party ad networks or user tracking frameworks.
